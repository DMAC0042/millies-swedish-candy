privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Millie's Swedish Candy Co. ("Millie's," "we," "us") runs this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use it, how we use that information, and the choices you have.

what we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through our forms:

  • Pickup orders: your name, phone number, email address, preferred pickup date and time, and the bags you'd like.
  • Event inquiries: your name, phone number, email address, event date, estimated guest count, venue, and the details you share about your event.

We don't ask for payment details on this website, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

how we use it

  • To prepare your pickup order and confirm it with you.
  • To reply to your event inquiry with pricing and details.
  • To contact you about your order or event, including if something changes.

We don't use your information for anything else, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service that delivers our form submissions to us, which handles it only to provide that service.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers and our business.

other services on this site

  • Fonts: our lettering is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Links: "get directions" opens Google Maps, and our Instagram links open Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Map: the shop map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data and is part of our own website, so viewing it doesn't send anything to a map provider.

how long we keep it

We keep order and inquiry details only as long as we need them to fill your order, plan your event, and keep ordinary business records. After that, we delete them.

keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No website or email system is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive information (such as payment card numbers) through our forms.

your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information you've sent us. Just reach out using the contact details below and we'll take care of it.

children

This website is meant for adults placing orders and planning events. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
