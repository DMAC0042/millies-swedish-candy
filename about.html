about millie's

from pop-ups to college park.

I grew up in College Park, and my family used to drive up this same road to grab candy from 7-Eleven or Blockbuster before movie nights. I always dreamed of having a candy store, and I still can't believe it's on the same street that holds all of my childhood memories.

What began as a little pop-up at local markets has grown into something so special: a place where people can discover the joy of Swedish candy, from sour gummies to salty licorice and everything in between.

Every bag is picked with love, and every customer feels like family. Whether you're a fan of Scandinavian sweets or trying your first sour skull, I hope Millie's can be that same sweet stop for the next generation.

Welcome to Millie's. I'm so glad you're here.

– millie
